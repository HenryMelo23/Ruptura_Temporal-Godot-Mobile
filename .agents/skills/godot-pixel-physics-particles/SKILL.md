# Godot Pixel Physics Particles

Use lightweight procedural systems first. For many particles, control lifetime, pooling, draw order, culling, and update cost. Prefer spatial partitioning when many bodies need proximity checks.

For mobile, cap concurrent effects, expose quality levels when needed, and preserve the effect silhouette on low quality.

Avoid default round particles as final art. Use shards, streaks, arcs, droplets, smoke puffs, tendrils, cracks, and temporal fragments that fit Ruptura Temporal.
